# 🧪 Starter Phase Chemistry System

## Overview

Players start with **lkC (Carbon)** from walking and unlock **lkO (Oxygen)**, **lkH (Hydrogen)**, and **lkCa (Calcium)** during the starter phase. The ultimate goal is to create **CaCO₃ (Calcium Carbonate)** as a basic construction material.

---

## 🎯 Starter Phase Elements

### Starting Element
- **lkC (Carbon)** - Obtained from walking (50m = 1 chunk → 12-20 raw lkC)

### Unlockable Elements (Starter Phase)
- **lkO (Oxygen)** - Unlock via pseudo-nuclear reaction (you'll design)
- **lkH (Hydrogen)** - Unlock via pseudo-nuclear reaction (you'll design)
- **lkCa (Calcium)** - Unlock via pseudo-nuclear reaction (you'll design)

---

## ⚗️ Real Chemical Reactions

### Basic Compounds (Building Blocks)

#### 1. **Water Formation** (Most Important!)
```
2 lkH + lkO → H₂O (Water)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 85%
- **Importance:** Essential for most other reactions
- **Real Chemistry:** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

#### 2. **Carbon Dioxide Formation**
```
lkC + 2 lkO → CO₂ (Carbon Dioxide)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 80%
- **Importance:** Needed for calcium carbonate
- **Real Chemistry:** C + O₂ → CO₂

#### 3. **Calcium Oxide Formation**
```
2 lkCa + lkO → 2 CaO (Quicklime)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 75%
- **Importance:** Intermediate for calcium carbonate
- **Real Chemistry:** 2Ca + O₂ → 2CaO

### Intermediate Compounds

#### 4. **Calcium Hydroxide Formation** (Slaked Lime)
```
CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ (Calcium Hydroxide)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 90%
- **Importance:** Pathway to calcium carbonate
- **Real Chemistry:** CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
- **Real Use:** Used in construction, mortar

#### 5. **Methane Formation** (Fuel!)
```
lkC + 2 lkH → CH₄ (Methane)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 70%
- **Importance:** Energy source, fuel
- **Real Chemistry:** C + 2H₂ → CH₄

#### 6. **Carbonic Acid Formation** (Weak Acid)
```
CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃ (Carbonic Acid)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (1⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 95%
- **Importance:** Natural reaction, intermediate
- **Real Chemistry:** CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃
- **Note:** This reaction naturally occurs (carbonation)

### Advanced Compounds

#### 7. **GOAL: Calcium Carbonate** (Construction Material!) 🎯
**Method A: From Calcium Hydroxide**
```
Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (3⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 85%
- **Importance:** PRIMARY CONSTRUCTION MATERIAL
- **Real Chemistry:** Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O
- **Real Use:** Limestone, marble, chalk, cement

**Method B: Direct from Calcium Oxide**
```
CaO + CO₂ → CaCO₃
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 80%
- **Importance:** Alternative pathway
- **Real Chemistry:** CaO + CO₂ → CaCO₃

#### 8. **Acetylene Formation** (Advanced Fuel)
```
2 lkC + lkH → C₂H₂ (Acetylene)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (3⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 60%
- **Importance:** High-energy compound, welding
- **Real Chemistry:** 2C + H₂ → C₂H₂

#### 9. **Calcium Hydride** (Hydrogen Storage)
```
lkCa + lkH → CaH₂ (Calcium Hydride)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (2⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 70%
- **Importance:** Hydrogen storage material
- **Real Chemistry:** Ca + H₂ → CaH₂
- **Real Use:** Desiccant, hydrogen source

#### 10. **Formic Acid** (Preservative)
```
lkC + lkH + lkO → HCOOH (Formic Acid)
```
- **Type:** Chemical Reaction (3⚡)
- **Success Rate:** 65%
- **Importance:** Simplest carboxylic acid
- **Real Chemistry:** Complex synthesis
- **Real Use:** Preservative, antibacterial

## 🎮 Gameplay Progression

### Phase 1: Unlocking Elements (Nuclear Reactions)
Players use **pseudo-nuclear reactions** (you'll design) to unlock:
1. **lkO** - From lkC (you design the formula)
2. **lkH** - From lkC or lkO (you design)
3. **lkCa** - From lkC (you design)

### Phase 2: Basic Compounds
Once unlocked, players discover:
1. **H₂O (Water)** - Essential for life and reactions
2. **CO₂ (Carbon Dioxide)** - Needed for construction
3. **CaO (Calcium Oxide)** - Building block

### Phase 3: Construction Material
Players combine compounds to create:
1. **Ca(OH)₂** - Intermediate step
2. **CaCO₃** - GOAL! Basic construction material

### Phase 4: Advanced Materials (Post-Starter)
Unlock more complex compounds:
- **CH₄ (Methane)** - Fuel
- **C₂H₂ (Acetylene)** - Advanced fuel
- **CaH₂** - Storage
- **HCOOH** - Chemical

## 🎲 Discovery System

### First Discovery
When a player creates a compound for the first time globally:
1. **Blockchain record** - NFT minted with discoverer's name
2. **Tax-free period** - 72 hours of 0% creation tax
3. **Royalties** - Earn 2× during tax period (10% from others)

### Subsequent Creators
After discovery:
1. **10% tax** - Goes to treasury
2. **2% to discoverer** - Rewards innovation
3. **Public formula** - Recipe becomes visible to all

## 🔬 Chemical Accuracy

All reactions are based on **real chemistry**:
- ✅ Stoichiometry is accurate
- ✅ Products are real compounds
- ✅ Energy requirements make sense
- ✅ Success rates reflect reaction difficulty

**Simplified for gameplay:**
- Single atoms instead of molecules (lkC vs C₁₂)
- Instant reactions (no reaction time)
- Pseudo-nuclear element unlocking
- 100% yield (no waste products)

## 📈 Balancing

### Element Availability (from walking)
- **lkC:** Very common (walking = guaranteed)
- **lkO:** Uncommon (1/10 nuclear success)
- **lkH:** Uncommon (1/10 nuclear success)
- **lkCa:** Rare (1/20 nuclear success)

### Compound Value
- **H₂O:** Low (common, easy to make)
- **CO₂:** Medium (needs 2 lkO)
- **CaO:** Medium-High (needs rare lkCa)
- **Ca(OH)₂:** High (multi-step synthesis)
- **CaCO₃:** Very High (construction material!)
